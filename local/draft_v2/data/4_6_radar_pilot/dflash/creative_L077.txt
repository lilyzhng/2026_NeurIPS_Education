**Item #: SCP-XXXX**  
**Object Class:** Euclid  

**Special Containment Procedures:**  
SCP-XXXX is not directly containable. All instances of SCP-XXXX-1 (the unmarked doors) are to be documented and monitored for activity. If a door is found in an abandoned building, it is to be sealed with reinforced concrete and monitored via remote cameras. Personnel entering SCP-XXXX-1 are restricted to Level 3 clearance or higher, and must be accompanied by at least two researchers. No unauthorized exploration is permitted.  

**Description:**  
SCP-XXXX refers to a series of identical unmarked doors (designated SCP-XXXX-1) discovered in abandoned buildings worldwide. Each door is 2.1 meters tall, 1.2 meters wide, and composed of an unknown, non-reflective material. No markings, handles, or mechanisms are present. SCP-XXXX-1s have been reported in locations ranging from derelict hospitals in Antarctica to collapsed subway stations in Tokyo, with no discernible pattern in their distribution.  

When opened (via unknown means), SCP-XXXX-1 leads to a vast, architecturally impossible archive designated SCP-XXXX-2. The archive defies Euclidean geometry, with corridors that loop infinitely, staircases that ascend into ceilings, and rooms that appear to exist in multiple dimensions simultaneously. The structure is estimated to span over 100 kilometers in all directions, though no physical boundaries exist.  

Documents recovered from SCP-XXXX-2 reference a "previous global civilization" known as the "Era of the First Convergence." This civilization, according to the records, was destroyed by a "containment failure" that resulted in the collapse of their reality. The archive appears to be a repository of their knowledge, including blueprints, historical records, and warnings about the dangers of "uncontained anomalies."  

Foundation researchers have discovered references to their own organization within SCP-XXXX-2. A 19th-century document titled *The Foundation’s Role in the Convergence* states:  
> "The Foundation was founded to prevent the recurrence of the Convergence. Its members are the last custodians of the Archive, bound by the oath to protect the knowledge of the First Civilization. Should the Archive be breached, the world shall be unmade."  

Further analysis revealed personnel files for current Foundation staff, including Dr. ██████ and Dr. █████, dated over 200 years prior. These files contained detailed biographies, psychological evaluations, and even handwritten notes from the researchers themselves.  

**Addendum XXXX-1: Exploration Log 1**  
*Date: ██/██/20██*  
*Team: RESEARCHER-7, RESEARCHER-12, RESEARCHER-19*  

Upon entering SCP-XXXX-2, the team encountered a section of the archive labeled "Sector 47-B." The walls were lined with shelves containing thousands of sealed containers, each labeled with a cryptic alphanumeric code. One container, labeled "E-13," contained a document titled *The Convergence Protocol.*  

> "The Convergence was not an accident. It was a failure of containment. The First Civilization sought to harness the power of the Archive, but their hubris led to the collapse of their reality. The Archive is not a repository—it is a prison. The entities within are not the threat; the Archive itself is."  

The team also discovered a series of footprints leading away from Sector 47-B. Upon following them, they encountered a group of entities resembling hazmat teams. These entities wore full-body suits with respirators and carried tools resembling containment equipment. When approached, the entities fled, leaving behind a trail of discarded gear.  

**Addendum XXXX-2: Exploration Log 2**  
*Date: ██/██/20██*  
*Team: RESEARCHER-7, RESEARCHER-12, RESEARCHER-19*  

Further investigation revealed that portions of SCP-XXXX-2 were being actively removed. A section of the archive labeled "Sector 12-A" had been partially excavated, with walls replaced by a void of "unstable reality." The team recorded a voice log from an unknown source:  

> "We are the Custodians. The Archive is not meant to be fully accessed. The Convergence is inevitable. We must ensure the Archive’s secrets do not reach the surface."  

The team also discovered a hidden chamber containing a holographic projection of an O5 Council member, Dr. ██████, who stated:  
> "The Archive is a relic of the First Civilization. We have been monitoring it for decades. The Foundation’s existence is tied to the Archive’s survival. If the Archive is breached, the world will end."

**Addendum XXXX-4: O5 Council Report**  
*Date: ██/██/20██*  
*Author: O5-█*  

The O5 Council has authorized covert operations to investigate SCP-XXXX-2. A team of 12 personnel, including O5-█, entered the archive in 20██. Their mission was to assess the risk of the Archive’s breach and determine whether the Foundation’s existence is tied to the Archive’s survival.  

The team reported encountering the Custodians, who attempted to prevent their access to certain sections of the archive. O5-█ stated:  
> "The Archive is not a threat. It is a warning. The Foundation’s role is to ensure the Archive remains sealed. The Convergence is not an accident—it is a cycle. The First Civilization failed. We must not fail."  

The team was last seen exiting the archive, but no further reports have been received.  

**Addendum XXXX-5: Incident Report**  
*Date: ██/██/20██*  
*Author: RESEARCHER-7*  

On ██/██/20██, a section of SCP-XXXX-2 collapsed, resulting in the deaths of three researchers. The incident was attributed to the Custodians, who had been actively removing sections of the archive. The collapse revealed a chamber containing a single, unmarked door labeled "SCP-XXXX-3."  

The door was immediately sealed, and further exploration of SCP-XXXX-2 has been suspended pending O5 approval.  

**Conclusion:**  
SCP-XXXX-2 represents a direct link to the First Civilization’s downfall. The Foundation’s existence is intertwined with the Archive’s survival, and the risk of the Convergence remains unresolved. Further research is restricted to O5-level authorization.
