At 1.0 the verifier runs exact rejection sampling: the output provably follows the target model, whatever the draft proposes. Below 1.0 the lossless guarantee is gone: draft-preferred tokens slip through, and the output distribution drifts toward the draft, up to the ceiling DLK(p, q). What we measured: on this task the label accuracy holds flat even at 0.3. A one-token answer gives the leak exactly one chance to flip a high-confidence position, so the damage hides. That is the trap: the guarantee is already gone, the metric just cannot see it yet. Long-form domains, where every position is a content position, are where the leak lands (see Figure 10's ceilings: creative writing 0.95).
